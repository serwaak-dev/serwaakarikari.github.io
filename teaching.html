Teaching

Rigorous, ethical, and applied marketing education.

I design learning experiences that connect behavioral theory with contemporary marketing decisions and societal impact.

Teaching philosophy

My teaching emphasizes evidence-based reasoning, ethical judgment, and active application. Students learn to interpret behavioral evidence, question assumptions, and use marketing tools responsibly.

Guest speakers, case analysis, collaborative projects, and contemporary market examples help bridge conceptual learning and professional practice.
